Meet Meredith
& Meagan

Meredith and Meagan are from the Quad Cities area of Illinois and have been workout partners for the past 15 years. Health and fitness have shaped how they live, and after years of talking about starting a business together, they decided it was time.

M² Energy is the result, named for the two of them: Meredith and Meagan, M squared. Their goal is simple: serve a healthier, safer alternative to traditional energy drinks without giving up flavor. Every drink starts with a Lotus base and is mixed to order with fruit flavors and creams.

For now, they’re bringing M² Energy to local events, horse shows, and community gatherings, meeting new people and showing them there’s a better way to get their caffeine. The long-term plan is a storefront of their own.
